# Insight (DR DR26-11-09-HUB-02): Verification of Nov 9, 2026 Lisbon 'Build Your Second Brain with Claude' workshop and comparators

> Does public evidence confirm that the Nov. 9, 2026 Portugal Tech Week workshop 'Build Your Second Brain with Claude' by Gui Perdrix (Timeback) happened as advertised, how does it compare with other 2026 Claude second-brain workshops, and what would a verifiable 90-minute beginner pilot look like?

## Ключевые выводы
- The Lisbon workshop is a well-substantiated real, publicly advertised event: the Portugal Tech Week event page, PTW Luma calendar and Luma ID sknzoqn5 agree on title, host Gui Perdrix, venue PTW Palace (Rua Gomes Freire 98), 10:00-11:30, free entry, 50 seats. A 351 Portuguese Startup Association Meetup mirror repeats the details. Confidence >95% for existence/date/capacity/syllabus.
- Registration was about full: the Luma calendar showed Waitlist with +49, and the Dealflow index said '50 registered'. These are registration-demand figures, not attendance. No public post-event headcount, recap, recording or slide deck for the Lisbon session was found (low confidence / unavailable for attendance).
- Gui Perdrix's identity is corroborated: Timeback's site and LinkedIn list him as co-founder/instructor (with Gabriella Mifsud), Timeback founded March 2026 near Lisbon; Portugal Startup News reported it in April 2026; Co-Liv's directory confirms him as president 2020-2024 and current board member. Minor inconsistency: his personal site says Timeback 'since 2025' versus March 2026 elsewhere, so exact founding date is moderate confidence.
- Advertised content (local folder synced to Google Drive, connect Claude, add context, project/task manager) is not the same as demonstrated delivery. Gui's pre-event LinkedIn video shows a folder/wiki/Obsidian graph/testimonial-query system, but is not evidence that these exercises were done in the Lisbon room. No Anthropic affiliation is claimed in the event copy; affiliation is with Portugal Tech Week only.
- Marketing claims remain unverified: Timeback's '23 hours a week back' and '1,200+ professionals trained', plus testimonial claims on hours saved. No methodology or audit was found. The promo phrase 'perfect memory of you' is a technical overstatement: CLAUDE.md is persistent instructions within a finite context window, and Anthropic advises keeping it lean.
- The 'Claude as second brain' format was already established in 2026: Munich Jan 26 (slides published, ~120 attendees); Copenhagen Jun 11 (12 went, capacity 12); Barcelona Sep 21 demo with public starter repo; Barcelona Sep 23 hands-on (137 went, sold out); public videos ranged ~8,108 to 118,433 views (Cole Medin 118,433; Matheus Battisti 84,183; Sebastian Claes 30,609; Kacper Sieradzinski 8,108). Luma 'Went' figures are the cleanest attendance proxies. The Barcelona Claude Community House totals (2,000+ people, 42 sessions, 3,600+ bookings) are program-wide, not per session.
- No Wayback/Internet Archive snapshot was found for the PTW or Luma URLs; the best snapshot-like evidence is PrompTugal.news, which says it read the PTW page on Nov. 1, 2026 (secondary copy only).
- Evidence-based beginner design starts simple, not with agents/MCP/Obsidian/Drive sync: plain CLAUDE.md, reference files instead of pasting, keep it lean and specific, then skills. The recommended 90-minute pilot uses a synthetic folder (about-me.md, project.md, style.md, meeting-notes.md, CLAUDE.md) with three falsifiable exercises: manually pick 5 stable facts, convert 3 style examples to rules, and keep volatile facts in meeting-notes.md; each is verified in a fresh session, plus a cold-start audit.

## Рекомендации / решения
- Treat the Lisbon event as verified for existence, schedule, venue, capacity and advertised syllabus, but report it as 'about 50 registrations / full 50-seat event', never as confirmed attendance.
- Keep registration, waitlist, capacity, attendance and views as separate fields in any comparative record; use Luma 'Went' counts (Barcelona 137, Copenhagen 12) as the cleaner attendance comparators.
- Treat Timeback efficacy and training-volume numbers and the 'perfect memory' claim as unverified commercial claims; do not repeat them as facts.
- Run the narrower 90-minute pilot (no Drive sync, no agents, no MCP, no Obsidian requirement, synthetic non-sensitive data) with the three exercises and fresh-session verification, and extend to Obsidian, Drive, skills, MCP and automation only after participants pass the tests.
- Prefer pilot success criteria that are inspectable (open the files, rerun the cold-start test) rather than screenshots of a model response.
