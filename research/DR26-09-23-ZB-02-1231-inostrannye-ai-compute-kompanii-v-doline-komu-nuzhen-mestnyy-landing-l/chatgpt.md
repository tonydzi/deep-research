The public record is much worse on failed individual landing-lead hires than on successful appointments. I would not invent named departures or attribute a company's US outcome to one employee without evidence. One useful company-level warning is DeepL: reporting in May 2026 said the company cut about 21% of its workforce, roughly 250 people, even while proceeding with its San Francisco expansion.
