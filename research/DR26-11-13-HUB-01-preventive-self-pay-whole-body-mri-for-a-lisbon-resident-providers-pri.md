# Insight (DR DR26-11-13-HUB-01): Preventive self-pay whole-body MRI for a Lisbon resident: providers, prices, evidence

> Compares eight Portuguese, German and French providers of self-pay preventive whole-body MRI for an asymptomatic Lisbon resident (price, protocol, referral rules, hardware, reputation) and weighs the clinical evidence for such screening.

## Ключевые выводы
- Practical ranking for a Lisbon resident: 1) B-Life/Primum (Oeiras), 2) NRD Lisbon, 3) CRMA Faro, 4) Imagem Diagnóstica Braga, 5) radprax Düsseldorf. B-Life/Primum is the easiest high-touch local option, NRD the best value, CRMA the best-specified Portuguese DWI protocol, radprax the best-documented no-referral foreign option.
- B-Life/Primum: €2,490 package (MRI plus carotid/thyroid/neck ultrasound, pre- and post-radiologist consultations, report). Its separate menu lists whole-body MRI at €1,900. MRI is performed at Primum, Oeiras, on a Siemens MAGNETOM Sempra 1.5 T. Regions are explicitly abbreviated, not dedicated exams. No IV contrast, DWI not specified, external-referral rule unknown. B-Life (NIPC 517643626, mapped to Marés Encantadas, Lda) and Primum (NIPC 517693909) are separate legal entities.
- NRD Lisbon: €500, about 60 min, result within 7 days, Philips Ingenia 1.5 T, covers skull to thighs (brain, spine, thorax, abdomen/pelvis, skeleton). Medical prescription is always required. DWI only 'in some cases', no dedicated prostate protocol. ERS issued an instruction in August 2023 after a patient was handed another patient's MRI envelope. Portal da Queixa shows 8 complaints since 2018.
- CRMA Faro has the most explicit Portuguese preventive protocol: vertex to mid-thigh, T1 Dixon + STIR/T2 + DWI, no routine contrast, MY-RADS/ONCO-RADS-oriented reporting, new Philips MR 5300 BlueSeal 1.5 T (in use from Sep 2026). It runs its own clinical assessment pathway. Price unpublished, Faro is far from Lisbon, and the prescription mechanics are unclear.
- Imagem Diagnóstica Braga: an ad dated 7 Apr 2026 stated about €950, about 1 hour and no attending-physician request needed. This could not be confirmed on a current official page, so price and no-referral status are unverified. Google rating is about 3.2/5 from 56 reviews, with phone-access complaints. JCC Porto lists 'RM Corpo Inteiro com Difusão', but price, referral rule and scanner are unknown. JCC was fined €3,000 by ERS in 2023 over advertising transparency, not clinical quality.
- Foreign options explicitly need no referral. radprax Düsseldorf: Siemens MAGNETOM Vida 3 T, MRI/MRA of about 1 h (head, organs, entire spine, arteries), €1,600 MRI Check or €2,550 Premium (with >60 lab values), appointments in about 2–3 weeks, contrast generally used, prostate is a paid extra. MAGNET Düsseldorf: from €1,180, about 50 min, GOÄ billing, contrast about €90–160 if needed. Predicto/IRM des Sources Lyon: from €2,000, no prescription, non-contrast, Philips 1.5 T, optional 56-biomarker panel by quote.
- 'Whole-body MRI' is not standardized. Protocols differ in brain, spine, DWI, prostate (only radprax and MAGNET state a dedicated prostate scan is not included or is an extra), vessels and contrast. Higher field strength (3 T) does not by itself prove better screening. Hidden gaps include lung micronodules, coronary arteries, bowel mucosa, breast and small superficial lesions.
- Society guidance (Ordem dos Médicos, ACR, RCR, RANZCR 2026) advises against whole-body MRI in asymptomatic average-risk people because there is no proven mortality or cost-effectiveness benefit and incidental findings are common. A 2025 meta-analysis of 9,024 asymptomatic people found a 1.57% confirmed cancer detection rate, and a 2026 meta-analysis of 18,043 people found about 14.8% had a non-malignant actionable finding. Of those with such findings, about 58% had further work-up and about 21% a procedure. False-positive and overdiagnosis rates could not be calculated. High-risk groups (predisposition syndromes, strong family history) are a different case.
- IV contrast is generally not recommended for routine asymptomatic screening, so a non-contrast default is not inferior. EMA restricts linear gadolinium agents and favours macrocyclic agents (gadobutrol, gadoteric acid, gadoteridol) at the lowest effective dose.

## Рекомендации / решения
- Choose NRD (€500) if price dominates and getting a prescription from a Lisbon GP or private consultation is acceptable. The roughly €1,990 gap to B-Life makes the consultation cost negligible.
- Choose B-Life/Primum for the easiest high-touch local experience, accepting its price and abbreviated protocol. Confirm the referral rule and DWI in writing first.
- Consider CRMA Faro if protocol quality (DWI, structured reporting) matters more than convenience. Ask for a price quote and the prescription mechanics.
- Reconfirm Braga's €950/no-referral terms directly before treating it as a cost/coverage compromise.
- For a deliberate medical trip with no referral and an MRA component, radprax Düsseldorf has the strongest published specification. MAGNET Düsseldorf is the cheaper alternative.
- Before paying, get written answers to eight questions: exact sequence-by-sequence protocol, whether prostate is dedicated mpMRI, anatomical limits and exclusions, referral requirement and whether an in-house physician can authorize it, contrast policy and agent, who acquires and reports (legal entity, radiologist registration, scanner), one all-in price, and incidental-finding triage and follow-up ownership.
- Treat the scan as an informed personal choice rather than evidence-based screening. Ask about structured reporting such as ONCO-RADS/MY-RADS and who owns follow-up if something is found.
