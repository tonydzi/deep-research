# DR26-08-01-MACANTON-02-658 — the exact prompt sent to every model

# DEEP RESEARCH — What a US frontier-AI-lab offer ACTUALLY pays a builder sitting in Lisbon

## Who is asking
A 45+ founder/engineer (physics PhD background, crypto/AI builder, runs a small independent research lab) living in **Lisbon, Portugal**, four children in school, currently ~zero salary.

**Target, stated explicitly:** to be hired by a **US company — Bay Area or New York** (Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, Google DeepMind US, Meta AI, Perplexity, Hugging Face US, or a serious AI-adjacent US company). **Working remotely is expected and fine** — these companies routinely employ people who are not in the office. **Relocation is NOT the goal.** He can and will fly to the Valley (or anywhere) periodically for on-sites, offsites, and conferences.

So this brief is NOT about emigrating a family of six. It is about one question:

> **A US company decides to hire him while he stays in Lisbon. Mechanically, how does that happen — and what number actually lands in his bank account?**

Every prior report we hold answers "how do we get NOTICED". None answers this. That is the gap.

## What we need answered, with evidence, links and dates

**1. The mechanics of employing a remote person abroad.**
- What are the actual instruments a US company uses to pay someone resident in Portugal: independent contractor (W-8BEN, invoicing from a PT sole trader / *trabalhador independente* or a PT company), Employer-of-Record (Deel, Remote.com, Velocity Global, Globalization Partners), a company's own EU/PT entity, or "we don't do that, relocate or no deal"?
- **Which of these do the named AI labs specifically use in 2026?** Evidence from job postings, careers-page fine print, employee accounts, Blind/Levels/Reddit threads, LinkedIn profiles of people employed by them from outside the US. Name names where documented.
- Which labs have a hard "US-only / must be in an approved country" policy, and which quietly hire internationally? Cite postings with dates.
- Does the answer differ by function — Research vs Applied/MTS vs DevRel/Developer Experience vs Community vs Solutions/Forward-Deployed? Which functions are the realistic door for a builder with public OSS output and no FAANG/ML-PhD track record?

**2. The money — US band or geo-cut?**
- Published 2025–2026 total-comp bands for the realistic roles (DevRel / Developer Experience / Applied AI / Solutions Architect / MTS-product) at these companies. Base vs equity vs bonus.
- **The decisive question: when they hire remote-international, do they pay the US band or geo-adjust it?** Get evidence, not theory — company policy statements, first-person accounts, EOR salary data. Quantify the haircut if there is one.
- **Contractor vs employee:** what does the same role pay as a contract engagement versus a payroll seat, and what does he lose (equity, benefits, health cover) as a contractor?
- **Equity that isn't stock:** Anthropic and OpenAI use non-standard instruments (PPUs, tender-offer liquidity). Explain how these work for a non-US person: can a foreign contractor even hold them, when do they become cash, what happens at a tender offer, and how is that taxed in Portugal.

**3. What actually lands, net, in Lisbon.**
- Portuguese tax treatment of income from a US employer/client for a PT tax resident: employee-via-EOR vs *trabalhador independente* vs own company. Effective rates including Social Security, with 2026 numbers.
- Status of **NHR / IFICI ("NHR 2.0") in 2026**: who still qualifies, what activities, what rate, and whether this profile can qualify. If the window is closed, say so plainly.
- US-Portugal double-taxation treaty in practice: withholding, W-8BEN, what he must file, common traps.
- **Benchmark to answer the real question:** what net annual EUR income does a family of six in Lisbon need for international-school fees + kids' sports + normal living? Give a sourced range, then state which of the comp scenarios above clears it and which does not.

**4. Travelling to the US for on-sites (not immigrating).**
- What status is needed for periodic business trips (offsites, onboarding weeks, conferences) — ESTA/VWP vs B-1 — and what activities are permitted on each while being paid by a US company as a foreign contractor/EOR employee. Where is the line that gets people refused at the border?
- Note: passport/citizenship determines VWP eligibility and we must confirm it separately — **cover the main scenarios (EU passport, PT residence permit only, third-country passport) rather than assuming one.**
- Do these companies sponsor/support business-visa letters for remote staff? Any documented practice.
- If a company later WANTS him onsite permanently: fastest realistic route (O-1 evidence bar for someone with publications + OSS + press, H-1B odds, L-1) — **one short section only**, this is the fallback, not the plan.

**5. The intermediate rungs that pay now.**
If a direct US offer is 12+ months away, what pays real money in the meantime and keeps the door open? Concretely, with 2026 amounts, mechanics and deadlines: maintainer/OSS grants (Linux Foundation & Alpha-Omega, Sovereign Tech Agency, NLnet, GitHub Accelerator/Sponsors, Open Collective), lab fellowship & grant programs (Anthropic Fellows / Economic Futures, OpenAI programs, xAI/Mistral equivalents), paid contractor work FOR the labs (data/evals/red-teaming vendors: Surge, Scale, Mercor, Handshake AI — rates, how to get in), and DevRel/contract roles at AI-adjacent US companies (LangChain, W&B, Modal, Replicate, Val Town, Continue…). For each: real money range, how you apply, and how strongly it converts into a full offer later.

## Rules of engagement
- **Cite everything with a date and a link.** Anything before mid-2025 must be flagged as possibly stale.
- Distinguish citation from inference — label inferences as hypotheses.
- **Do not give motivational advice.** No "build in public and they'll find you" — we already hold fifteen reports saying that. This is about instruments, numbers, and tax.
