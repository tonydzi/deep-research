# Insight (DR DR26-07-07-MACB-05): Positioning a founder-builder for a Developer Experience/Applied AI role at a frontier LLM company

> Analyzes which roles at Anthropic/OpenAI/Mistral/xAI/Google DeepMind actually fit a founder-builder profile without a PhD, what public hiring pattern converts, how to frame a crypto/web3 background, and lays out a 90-day portfolio plan to trigger a first conversation.

## Ключевые выводы
- The best-fit roles are not core research scientist but the DevRel/Developer Experience/Applied AI/Forward Deployed/Solutions Architecture band — several current listings explicitly accept 'bachelor's/equivalent practical experience' instead of a PhD.
- Anthropic's 'Applied AI Claude Evangelist, Startups' is the tightest match: wants 7+ years founding/building startups, builder credibility with founders/VCs/accelerators, production-quality demos, and even requires a public-speaking video in the application.
- At OpenAI the realistic targets are Developer Experience Engineer, Developer Education Lead, Technical Community Lead, and (as a stretch) Applied AI Engineer/Codex Core Agent.
- Mistral is the most reachable target from a Lisbon base (AI Developer Advocate, Developer Education Lead, Forward Deployed ML Engineer, Research Software Engineer) due to European geography and an open Discord/GitHub/ambassador community.
- The dominant public hiring pattern is 'artifact pull, not résumé push': Peter Steinberger (OpenClaw→OpenAI), Jason Liu (Instructor→OpenAI DevEx), Thomas Ricouard (Codex Monitor→OpenAI), and Anthropic's acquisitions of Stainless, Vercept, and Bun all followed a builder shipping a tool/product with real adoption, not a research essay.
- The single highest-leverage artifact for this candidate is an open-source, cross-model agent orchestration harness (multi-machine, memory-backed, evaluable, observable) that externalizes his private multi-agent Claude fleet practice — this matches exactly what target-company job descriptions ask for (demos, SDKs, evals, tooling).
- A rigorous benchmark/write-up is valuable mainly as an amplifier for the tool, not as the primary asset — converting hires were tool-first, not essay-first.
- Crypto/web3 background is a mild liability by default (Anthropic explicitly staffs against Fraud & Scams harms and had a wallet-drainer Discord-phishing incident in its own ecosystem) but becomes an asset if reframed from 'crypto founder/tokenomics' to 'operator in adversarial, low-trust, incentive-misaligned, security-sensitive systems' — which maps to evals, safeguards, cybersecurity, and production-deployment roles.
- Core research/'Research Lead' titles (e.g., Anthropic's Research Lead, Training Insights) are a low-probability near-term target since they require publication-level ML research or large-scale eval-science leadership.
- Visibility happens through each company's own official builder surfaces — OpenAI Developer Forum/Cookbook/Discord/hackathons/DevDay Exchanges (Paris, Berlin, London); Anthropic Discord/Claude Cookbooks/events/newsletter; Mistral Discord/GitHub/ambassador program — not through cold outreach.

## Рекомендации / решения
- Do not position as 'research lead' or research scientist — position as a research-adjacent builder living at the intersection of long-horizon agents, evals, and developer tooling.
- Ship a concrete open-source agent orchestration artifact (multi-machine, persistent memory, eval harness, observable traces) as the centerpiece portfolio piece within the next 90 days, following the week-by-week plan: repo skeleton (wk1-2) → working demo with cost/latency traces (wk3-4) → published eval/benchmark methodology (wk5-6) → contributions to a target company's cookbook/community (wk7-8) → polished packaging + build-in-public cadence (wk9-10) → a public talk/livestream (wk11-12) → surgical applications to 4-8 roles with the full artifact bundle attached (wk13).
- Reframe crypto/web3 experience explicitly away from tokens/community/growth language and toward 'operated software in adversarial markets with abuse pressure, incentive design, and security constraints' — never lead with crypto identity or ideology.
- Prioritize portfolio investments in this order: (1) a widely-useful open-source tool, (2) direct contributions to the target company's own OSS/community (cookbook PRs, Discord help, issue fixes), (3) a rigorous benchmark/write-up as a companion piece, (4) a build-in-public content cadence as distribution only, not as the primary artifact.
- Target Anthropic (Applied AI Claude Evangelist, Startups; Applied AI Architect, Partnerships), OpenAI (Developer Experience Engineer, Developer Education Lead, Technical Community Lead), Mistral (AI Developer Advocate, Developer Education Lead, Forward Deployed ML Engineer), and xAI (Developer Experience Engineer, Forward Deployed Engineer, X API) as the highest-probability role set.
- Use warm intros from the founder/VC network only after the artifact has independent social proof (external stars/PRs/reproductions) — cold outreach or intro requests before that point wastes social capital.
- Avoid over-claiming ('autonomous AI fleet') without reproducible evals, failure-mode documentation, permissions models, and cost/latency data — target companies hire against measurable activation and production reliability, not agent-swarm aesthetics.
- Become a known, genuinely useful presence in exactly one or two target-company community surfaces (OpenAI cookbook/forum, Anthropic Discord/cookbooks, or Mistral Discord/GitHub) rather than broadcasting broadly.

## Открытые вопросы
- Evidence of 'what converts' is weaker for Google DeepMind and xAI than for OpenAI/Anthropic, since those two have fewer publicized builder-hire case studies and rely more on job-board listings.
- Several hire-mechanism conclusions are inferred from personal sites, public posts, and acquisition announcements rather than confirmed formal recruiting retrospectives.
- Reported revenue/team-size details for Aman Gottumukkala's Firebender are sourced from press reporting, not company-confirmed, and should be treated cautiously.
- No official hiring rule confirms that 'tool-first beats essay-first' — this ranking is an inference from a small set of examples, not a stated policy.
- Unclear how much the crypto-liability framing generalizes beyond Anthropic's fraud/scams-focused hiring signal to OpenAI, Mistral, xAI, and Google specifically.
