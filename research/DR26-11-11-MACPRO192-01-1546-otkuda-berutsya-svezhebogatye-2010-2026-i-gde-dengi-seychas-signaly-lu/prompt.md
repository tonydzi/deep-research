# DR26-11-11-MACPRO192-01-1546 — Свежебогатые: сверхдоходные вертикали 2010-2026 по сигналам luxury-покупок (Дубай, суперкары, часы, яхты, джеты)

# Deep Research: Where do the "new rich" come from? Mapping super-profit verticals 2010–2026 through luxury-purchase signals

## Who is asking and why
An investigative-research lab wants a repeatable method to spot, early, the niches where people get rich fast (the way the 2016–2018 ICO boom and the 2020 DeFi Summer minted thousands of "fresh-rich" people from projects built almost "on a laptop"). The output feeds two decisions: (1) which verticals deserve a deeper journalistic investigation next, and (2) where the money is concentrating right now (2025–2026) and who receives it. Treat this as investigative journalism plus quantitative analysis, not a market-sizing brochure.

## Core idea to test
Newly made money shows up in conspicuous purchases before it shows up in official statistics. Track, year by year from 2010 to 2026, WHO (by source industry of wealth) became the marginal buyer of the most expensive goods:
- supercars (Lamborghini, Ferrari, Rolls-Royce, Bugatti, Pagani), incl. regional dealer data;
- ultra-luxury watches (Patek Philippe, Richard Mille, AP, Rolex secondary-market premiums);
- prime real estate, with **Dubai as a dedicated deep-dive** (DLD transaction data, $10M+ deals, off-plan booms, buyer nationalities and declared professions), plus Monaco, Miami, London, Singapore, Lisbon, Bali, Cyprus, Tbilisi, Bangkok as comparison;
- superyachts (Boat International / SuperYacht Times order books, owner industries);
- private jets and helicopters (fleet registries, Wingx/Jetnet/AMSTAT summaries, new-owner industries);
- trophy mansions and land.

## Two-metric framework (keep them separated in the output)
1. **Rumours / soft signals**: "this niche is extremely rich" (e.g. iGaming, affiliate marketing, crypto market-making, OnlyFans agencies, dropshipping, adult, arbitrage traffic, prop trading, AI tooling, GLP-1 / telehealth, bioengineering, nutra, MLM, sports betting, forex/CFD brokers, Telegram/TON, memecoin launchpads, real-money gaming in India, LatAm iGaming, Gulf contractors, sanctioned-trade intermediaries). Rumours are legitimate leads: list them with source type (forum, broker anecdote, press, leaked deck) and a confidence score.
2. **Purchase facts / semi-facts**: documented or reported purchases tied to an industry of origin ("buyers of $10M+ Dubai villas in 2022 were disproportionately crypto founders", "Richard Mille waitlists filled by iGaming affiliates"). Give counts, $ volumes, dates, and the source for each.

Then bridge to **hard numbers**: for each vertical, estimate revenue / profit pool per year 2010–2026, number of people who became multi-millionaires (if any proxy exists), and how much of that flowed into luxury assets.

## Required outputs
1. **Year × vertical heatmap 2010–2026**: for each year, the top 3–5 industries generating new luxury buyers, with the evidence strength (fact / semi-fact / rumour) and links.
2. **Dubai deep-dive**: who bought prime property each year 2015–2026 by industry/nationality, record deals, how the buyer mix shifted (Russian capital post-2022, crypto 2021–22 & 2024–25, Indian, iGaming/affiliate, AI 2025–26). Use DLD data, Knight Frank, Betterhomes, Property Monitor, Henley & Partners where possible.
3. **Low-profile verticals**: niches that are very rich but almost invisible publicly (who are they, how to detect them: company registries in Cyprus/Malta/Curaçao/Isle of Man, payment-processor data, conference attendance like SiGMA/ICE/Affiliate World, luxury-dealer anecdotes, visa programs).
4. **Leading-indicator playbook**: a concrete, repeatable monitoring method for 2026+ (which datasets, dealer reports, registries, conferences, social signals, wealth reports: Knight Frank Wealth Report, Henley Private Wealth Migration, UBS Billionaire Report, Altrata/Wealth-X, Capgemini World Wealth; luxury earnings calls from Ferrari, Richemont, LVMH, Swatch; Chrono24/WatchCharts price indices). For each signal: lag vs. the boom, cost to collect, false-positive risk.
5. **Where is the money now (2025–2026)**: ranked list of currently hot verticals with evidence, who receives the money (role archetypes: founders, affiliates, operators, market-makers, early employees), and entry barriers.
6. **Investigation candidates**: top 5 verticals worth a journalistic deep-dive next, each with the key open question and the first 3 sources to pull.

## Rules
- Period 2010–2026 inclusive; mark anything after mid-2026 as preliminary.
- Every number needs a source link and a date; estimates are labelled "estimate" with the method. Never fill gaps with plausible numbers.
- Separate correlation from causation (e.g. Dubai booms are also driven by visa policy, sanctions, tax).
- Cover non-English sources (Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Portuguese, Chinese) where the money actually talks.
- Neutral, non-judgemental tone on grey verticals (gambling, adult, crypto); flag legal status per jurisdiction instead of moralising.
- Finish with: strongest counter-argument to the "luxury purchases reveal new-money verticals" thesis, and what evidence would falsify it.
