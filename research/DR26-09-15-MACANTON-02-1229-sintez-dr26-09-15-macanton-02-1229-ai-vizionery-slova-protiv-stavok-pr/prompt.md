# Deep Research: AI visionaries in Andrej Karpathy's league — theses, personal investments, and what they collectively predict for 2026–2030

## Context
I am an independent AI builder (Palo Alto AI Research Lab) whose goal is to get noticed by and hired into a top LLM company. I need an evidence-based map of the ~20 most influential technical AI "visionaries" (people whose public statements move how engineers and investors think), what they claim, where they put their own money, and where their predictions agree or conflict. A preliminary desk scan (Sept 2026) found, among other things: Karpathy joined Anthropic's pre-training team on 2026-05-19; Sutskever says "the age of scaling is over, back to the age of research"; LeCun's AMI Labs raised $1.03B for world models; Amodei called for slowing development on 2026-09-13; Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness fund was long power/memory and short chipmakers, then deleveraged in July 2026. Verify or refute these — do not trust them.

## Questions
1. **Roster.** Who are the 20 most influential technical AI visionaries as of September 2026 (builders/researchers, not just commentators)? Include Karpathy, Sutskever, Hassabis, Amodei, Altman, LeCun, Hinton, Fei-Fei Li, Suleyman, Murati/Schulman, Sutton, Chollet, Andrew Ng, Aschenbrenner, Kokotajlo, Noam Brown, Jensen Huang, Musk — and name who is MISSING from this list (e.g., Chinese labs: Liang Wenfeng/DeepSeek, Yang Zhilin/Moonshot; Europe: Arthur Mensch; others like Jeff Dean, Demis-adjacent, Jan Leike, Chris Olah, Tri Dao). For each: current role (2026), one-line why they matter.
2. **Theses.** For each person: their 3 most important claims about where AI is going (2025–2026 statements preferred), each with date, exact short quote, and URL. Their stated AGI/superintelligence timeline, and how that timeline has shifted over the last 2 years.
3. **Camps.** Cluster them into camps (e.g., scaling optimists, "LLMs are a dead end / new architecture", safety/slow-down, gradualists). Where do they explicitly disagree with each other (cite the public exchanges)?
4. **Money.** Personal investments, angel checks, funds, companies founded — with round size, valuation, date, source. Especially: Karpathy's verified angel portfolio (Perplexity, Inception, Simile, MatX, Flapping Airplanes — confirm, and find others), Altman (Helion, Oklo, Retro, Merge Labs, World), Nat Friedman/Daniel Gross, Elad Gil, Jeff Dean, Aschenbrenner's 13F holdings by quarter. Distinguish "said publicly" from "put own money in".
5. **Say vs. bet gap.** Where does a person's money contradict their public talk (e.g., bullish on AGI but short chipmakers; "slow down" but racing)? This is the most valuable section.
6. **Consensus forecast 2026–2030.** What does the group, weighted by track record, predict for: agents/coding, jobs (entry-level white-collar), robotics/world models, energy/compute bottlenecks, regulation, AGI date? Include a scorecard of past predictions by these people that already came true or failed (2020–2025).
7. **Actionable for an outsider builder.** Which of these people (or their teams/labs) are publicly reachable through open-source repos, issue trackers, fellowships, or hiring programs that actually respond? Which repos are active and merge outside contributions vs. dead queues? Give concrete evidence (merge rates, recent maintainer responses).
