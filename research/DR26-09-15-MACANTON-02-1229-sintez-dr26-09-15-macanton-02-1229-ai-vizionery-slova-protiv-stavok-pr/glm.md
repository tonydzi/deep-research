Members: Altman, Hassabis, Amodei, Musk, Huang, Brown (test-time compute variant)
Thesis: More compute + more data + better engineering → AGI. Brown's variant: "capability is now a function of inference budget" — scaling test-time compute (o1/o3, GPT-5 series) is the new frontier.

Camp 2: "LLMs Are a Dead End / New Architecture"

Members: LeCun, Sutskever, Sutton, Chollet
Thesis: Current LLMs lack world models, reasoning, and data efficiency. LeCun: "LLMs are a dead end" — must build world models (AMI Labs).
