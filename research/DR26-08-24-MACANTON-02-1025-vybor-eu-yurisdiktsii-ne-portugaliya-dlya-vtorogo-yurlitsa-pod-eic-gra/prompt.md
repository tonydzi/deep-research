# Deep Research: EU jurisdiction (Delaware-equivalent) for a second entity to unlock EU non-dilutive funding

## Context
We already have a **Delaware C-corp** (US venture / acqui-hire friendly). We want a **second EU/EEA legal entity** for two reasons:
1. **Access EU non-dilutive funding** — chiefly the **EIC Accelerator** (and Horizon Europe / EIC Pathfinder), which require the applicant to be an SME **established in an EU member state or associated country**. A Delaware corp does not qualify.
2. A **safe, founder-friendly, low-tax-risk home** comparable in reputation and predictability to Delaware.

**Hard constraint: NOT Portugal** (the founder resides in Lisbon but explicitly does not want a Portuguese entity due to tax-authority risk). The founder is willing to incorporate elsewhere in the EU/EEA and manage remotely.

**Company:** AI **agent-security** software — a product (agent control/audit, "agent-leash") with early **service revenue** (paid pilot-audits). Pre-seed stage, no external round yet.

## Questions (rank and justify)
Compare the realistic candidates — **Estonia (e-Residency), Ireland, Netherlands, Cyprus, Luxembourg, Lithuania, Malta**, and any other strong option — on:
1. **EIC / Horizon eligibility** — which cleanly satisfy "SME established in a member/associated state", and any nuance (e.g. genuine establishment / substance tests EIC applies).
2. **Corporate tax + effective burden** (headline + effective, incl. distributed-profit models like Estonia's).
3. **Setup speed, cost, and fully-remote formation & management** (e-Residency, remote directors, remote banking).
4. **Substance requirements** — can a small remote team run it without a physical office / local director, or is economic substance required?
5. **Banking / EU IBAN access** for a remote founder.
6. **Compatibility with the existing US Delaware parent** — cleanest holding structure (Delaware parent → EU sub, or EU sub standalone), IP location, transfer-pricing simplicity.
7. **CFC / tax-treaty risk given a Portugal-resident founder** — where does the founder's PT tax residence create controlled-foreign-company or permanent-establishment exposure, and which jurisdictions minimize it.
8. **Reputation & exit-friendliness for a US acquirer** (acqui-hire scenario) — which EU jurisdictions are painless in due diligence vs. which raise flags.

## Deliverable
A **ranked recommendation** (1st / 2nd / 3rd) with the tradeoff table, the specific EIC-eligibility gate each option satisfies, the **cleanest US-parent + EU-subsidiary structure**, and the concrete first steps + rough cost/time for the top pick. Flag anything that is jurisdiction-specific advice requiring a local tax lawyer.
