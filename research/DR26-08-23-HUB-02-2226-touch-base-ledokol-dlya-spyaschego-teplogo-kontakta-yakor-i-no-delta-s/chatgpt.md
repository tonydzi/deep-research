A. Executive Summary

The strongest evidence does not identify a magic phrase. It identifies a reconnection process: demonstrate accurate shared memory, update each other’s current state, and avoid assuming that the old relationship’s closeness, hierarchy, or interests are unchanged. In the closest controlled study, remembering the shared past, catching up, and showing a mutually accurate view of the current relationship independently increased willingness to help; current relationship alignment was the strongest factor. Catching up alone did not significantly increase willingness to meet. ([Rondi, Levin & De Massis, 24 May 2023](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/orsc.2023.1685)) [single-source]

Therefore, the first DM should normally be:

“[Name] — [specific, safely known shared anchor]. [One genuine reason this is relevant now]. Is [one bounded present-state question]?”

Use text in the existing thread, normally 2–4 short sentences and one question. The recommended 50–90-word band is operational, not scientifically optimized: no experiment was found that compares message lengths, questions versus statements, gap acknowledgment, humor, exact quotation, voice notes, or video specifically for dormant warm B2B DMs. [no source found]

The anchor hierarchy should be:

1. An unkept promise—only after completing or concretely repairing it.
2. Their unresolved problem, project, or question.
3. A shared event or piece of work that has present relevance.
4. A current trigger they knowingly made public or told you directly.
5. A legitimate mutual-contact context.
6. Your concrete update.
7. A purely relational memory, explicitly without a commercial ask.

No study compares these anchor types head-to-head. The ordering is a risk-adjusted operational judgment based on reconnection, trust-repair, advice-seeking, and privacy evidence.

Silence is not strictly better when there is no delta. Thirteen experiments involving more than 5,900 participants found that people systematically underestimate how much a distant contact will appreciate an unexpected note, particularly when the contact is surprised. That research measured appreciation—not replies, meetings, revenue, or durable B2B revival. ([Liu et al., 11 July 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35816566/)) [single-source] The correct policy is therefore two lanes:

- Commercial lane: no touch without a concrete delta, repaired obligation, real question, or relevant trigger.
- Relational lane: a rare, unmistakably agenda-free “thought of you” note is legitimate; it must not turn into a concealed pitch after they reply.

Do not automatically turn a response into a meeting. A revived relationship and a booked call are different outcomes. For engineers and other async-native contacts, the preferred conversion is often permission to send three bullets, an answer in-thread, a document/comment, an introduction, or an agreed revisit date. GitHub’s engineering communication guidance similarly treats chat and written artifacts as default coordination mechanisms and synchronous conversation as an escalation when async work is insufficient. ([GitHub Engineering, undated living document, accessed 24 August 2026](https://github.com/github/how-engineering-communicates/blob/main/how-github-engineering-communicates.md)) [single-source]

AI should retrieve and organize history, identify possible anchors, flag risks, and draft alternatives. The founder should choose the memory, certify that it is true and appropriate, provide the genuine “why now,” choose the ask, and approve the final text. Recipients are often poor at detecting AI authorship, but suspected or disclosed AI authorship can reduce perceived authenticity—especially for emotional messages. ([Jakesch, Hancock & Naaman, 7 March 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10089155/); [Hohenstein et al., 4 April 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-30938-9); [Kirk & Givi, 1 October 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbusres.2024.114984))

B. Key Findings

1. Reconnection is not a cold start, but it is not a continuation either

[established]

In a study of 224 executives, advice obtained from contacts dormant for at least three years compared favorably with advice from active contacts. Previously strong dormant ties combined novelty and efficiency commonly associated with weak ties with trust and shared perspective associated with strong ties. ([Levin, Walter & Murnighan, 30 September 2010](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/orsc.1100.0576)) [single-source]

The “three years” threshold was a study definition, not an experimentally discovered decay point. The authors’ follow-up analyses did not find materially different knowledge value between ties dormant for three years and those dormant longer, so there is no evidence-based date at which a dormant tie suddenly becomes equivalent to a stranger. ([Levin, Walter & Murnighan, 30 September 2010](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/orsc.1100.0576)) [single-source]

Operational implication: even after two years, preserve the shared history—but re-establish identity, present context, and the current relationship before making an ask.

2. The best direct evidence supports three moves: remember, update, recalibrate

[established]

Rondi, Levin, and De Massis combined observations and 71 interviews with a preregistered-style vignette experiment involving 331 US workers. In the experiment:

- Remembering the shared past increased estimated willingness to help from 4.54 to 5.08.
- Catching up increased it from 4.61 to 5.01.
- Perceiving the relationship similarly increased it from 4.37 to 5.25, the largest effect.
- Combining all three raised estimated willingness from 3.86 to 5.72.
- Catching up alone did not significantly increase meeting likelihood, although it increased the proposed meeting duration among those prepared to meet.
- Relational trust statistically mediated the effects.

([Rondi, Levin & De Massis, 24 May 2023](https://eprints.lancs.ac.uk/196191/1/OS_DEF.pdf)) [single-source]

“Perceiving the relationship similarly” included correctly understanding present tie strength, relative status, conflict, and competitive position—not merely remembering the past. ([Rondi, Levin & De Massis, 24 May 2023](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/orsc.2023.1685)) [single-source]

Operational implication: the opener should neither behave as if nothing changed nor over-explain the gap. It should make recognition easy and invite a small current-state correction.

3. There is almost no comparative evidence for exact opener copy

[established]

A 2025 study coded 863 draft notes to old friends across more than 20 textual characteristics. Questions, word count, memories, apologies, temporal orientation, and most other features did not consistently predict whether senders actually sent the note. Positive language and second-person language were associated with sending in one sample but not reliably across studies. The outcome was sender behavior—not recipient replies—so this study cannot tell us which copy revives a B2B thread. ([Castaneto & Aknin, 28 August 2025](https://online.ucpress.edu/collabra/article/11/1/143319/212890/Who-Reaches-Out-to-Old-Friends-and-What-Do-They)) [single-source]

No credible dormant-warm-DM experiment was found comparing:

- 30 versus 60 versus 100 words;
- a question versus a statement;
- naming versus not naming the communication gap;
- exact quotation versus paraphrase;
- humor or self-deprecation;
- text versus asynchronous voice note;
- text versus personalized video;
- English versus Russian wording.

[no source found]

Therefore, copy rules beyond the three-part reconnection process should be treated as testable operating hypotheses, not universal psychological laws.

4. Questions help conversations, but have not been shown to win the first reply

[emerging]

Across live dyadic-conversation studies, people who asked more questions—especially follow-up questions—were generally better liked by conversation partners. Those experiments concerned ongoing conversations, not the first DM after dormancy. ([Huang et al., 27 April 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28447835/)) [single-source]

The 2025 old-friend message study did not find a consistent relationship between including a question and actually sending the message, and it did not measure recipient response. ([Castaneto & Aknin, 28 August 2025](https://online.ucpress.edu/collabra/article/11/1/143319/212890/Who-Reaches-Out-to-Old-Friends-and-What-Do-They)) [single-source]

Recommendation: use one bounded question when you genuinely need current-state information. For pure goodwill, use no question and explicitly remove reply pressure.

5. Shared history works when it demonstrates recognition—not surveillance

[established]

The reconnection research describes remembering as identifying each other and jointly reconstructing meaningful shared experience. It also reports a failure case in which one person revealed unexpected knowledge about the other’s personal life; the recipient became overwhelmed and ended the interaction. ([Rondi, Levin & De Massis, 24 May 2023](https://eprints.lancs.ac.uk/196191/1/OS_DEF.pdf)) [single-source]

A platform experiment involving 666 dormant online ties found that messages containing suitable information about a friend’s activity could revive interaction, while unsuitable content could reverse the effect; more than half of revived ties maintained comparable engagement quality 2.5 months later. However, it involved automated platform messages and roughly one month of inactivity, not founder-written B2B DMs after 3–24 months. ([ICWSM study, 2013](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICWSM/article/download/14390/14239/17908)) [single-source]

Recommendation:

- Safe: “You mentioned you were testing channel partnerships.”
- Riskier: quoting an old sentence with transcript-like precision.
- Unsafe: “I saw you became active in three founder chats,” private family updates obtained elsewhere, or inferred personal events.

6. A no-delta message can create goodwill, but not proven pipeline value

[established]

The “surprise of reaching out” experiments show that recipients appreciate unexpected contact more than senders predict. Some experiments included only a short note; others included a small gift. Greater surprise and greater social distance increased the appreciation gap. ([Liu et al., 11 July 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35816566/)) [single-source]

An older holiday-greeting study found weak support for routine greetings as active relationship maintenance; personalized messages mattered more, and the authors suggested generic greetings might function as a “hygiene factor”—their absence may be noticed more than their presence creates value. This is old, observational evidence and not B2B messaging. ([Dindia et al., October 2004](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0265407504045888)) [single-source]

Verdict:

- “Nothing new, but I genuinely thought of you” can be legitimate relational maintenance.
- “Happy holidays” sent at scale is not an evidence-backed revival strategy.
- A no-delta message should not contain or immediately conceal a commercial ask.
- If the intended outcome is pipeline conversion rather than goodwill, silence remains preferable until a legitimate anchor appears.

7. Asking for an opinion is legitimate only when the expertise match is real

[established]

Experiments on advice-seeking found that asking for advice can increase perceived competence when the problem is difficult and the recipient is genuinely seen as an expert; it can backfire when the recipient lacks the relevant expertise. ([Brooks, Gino & Schweitzer, 2015](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/Advice%20Seeking_59ad2c42-54d6-4b32-8517-a99eeae0a45c.pdf)) [single-source]

Recommendation: “A or B—what is your one-line instinct?” is a valid no-news anchor only if the decision is real, the person is unusually qualified, and a one-line answer would actually help. Do not manufacture “I value your opinion” as disguised lead generation.

8. Voice creates connection, but first-touch voice notes remain untested

[emerging]

Experiments comparing voice-bearing communication with text found that voice produced stronger feelings of social connection without the expected increase in awkwardness. One field experiment involved old friends speaking by phone; the work did not test unsolicited asynchronous voice notes. ([Kumar & Epley, 10 September 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32915017/)) [single-source]

A large observational telehealth study associated provider voice notes with greater patient gratitude, intended adherence, gifts, and repeat consultation, but it was neither a dormant-tie study nor a randomized voice-note experiment. ([SSRN paper, posted 6 August 2024, revised 5 July 2026](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4912227)) [single-source]

Recommendation:

- Default to text for the first re-entry.
- Use voice first only when voice messages were already normal in that relationship.
- Otherwise move to voice after a positive reply.
- Treat 30–45 seconds as an operational cap, not a research-derived optimum.
- There is no credible basis for defaulting to personalized video. [no source found]

9. Humor is high variance; shared humor is safer than performed charm

[emerging]

Across eight experiments, successful, appropriate humor increased perceived status through confidence and competence, while inappropriate humor reduced perceived competence and status. The studies were not about dormant relationships. ([Bitterly, Brooks & Schweitzer, 10 November 2016](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/Bitterly%20Brooks%20Schweitzer%20JPSP%202016_54efbab5-2561-4408-b008-38d958e0ad50.pdf)) [single-source]

Recommendation: use humor only as a callback to humor already established between the two people. Do not use self-deprecation to apologize for existing—“sorry to crawl out of the woodwork”—or to soften a large ask.

10. Trigger-based reconnection has recent evidence; fixed cadence does not

[emerging]

A 2025 longitudinal study examined approximately 2.1 million posts and interactions from a nationally representative Chinese Gen-Z cohort over three years. Major life events were associated with dormant-tie reactivation; interaction later declined but remained active for at least six months. The setting was social media and major life transitions, not private B2B prospecting. ([Zhao et al., November 2025](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0747563225001931)) [single-source]

No controlled evidence was found showing that a quarterly, seasonal, or annual “touch base” cadence outperforms waiting for meaningful triggers among dormant warm B2B contacts. [no source found]

11. Age of silence changes the amount of orientation, not the relationship’s existence

[emerging]

The available research supplies no empirically validated breakpoint at one, six, twelve, or twenty-four months. Even ties dormant for three years or more retained useful shared perspective in the executive study. ([Levin, Walter & Murnighan, 30 September 2010](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/orsc.1100.0576)) [single-source]

Recommended heuristic:

- One month: continue the conversation; do not dramatize the gap.
- Three to six months: light re-entry—one anchor, one delta, one question.
- Six to twenty-four months: reorient—name the original context, update present relevance, allow for changed priorities.
- More than twenty-four months, or major role/company change: use a warm re-introduction.
- Regardless of time: if the prior interaction was thin enough that recognition is uncertain, identify yourself and the context.

These bands are [speculative]; no causal study validates them.

12. AI detectability is less important than authenticity and factual integrity

[emerging]

Across six experiments involving about 4,600 participants, people were only around chance at detecting AI-generated self-presentation in professional, hospitality, and dating contexts. The study predates the newest models and did not test short dormant-contact DMs. ([Jakesch, Hancock & Naaman, 7 March 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10089155/)) [single-source]

Two experiments with 1,036 participants found that AI-assisted replies increased speed and positive emotional language, but people evaluated partners more negatively when they suspected AI involvement, whether or not AI had actually been used. ([Hohenstein et al., 4 April 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-30938-9)) [single-source]

Six preregistered marketing experiments found an authenticity penalty when recipients believed emotional communications had been AI-written; the penalty was smaller for factual communication and when AI edited rather than originated the message. ([Kirk & Givi, 1 October 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbusres.2024.114984)) [single-source]

Two 2025 experiments on birthday greetings found AI-generated wishes were judged less sincere and effortful; personalization mitigated the penalty, and established closeness buffered it. This was social rather than B2B communication. ([Zhang, Liu & Li, 29 December 2025](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/1369118X.2025.2606100)) [single-source]

The standing rule should therefore be: AI may prepare the message; it must not invent or impersonate the relationship.

C. Contradictions & Debates

1. “Never send without a delta” versus “people appreciate being remembered”

Both positions are defensible because they optimize different outcomes.

The strongest case for no-delta contact is relational: recipients appreciate unexpected contact more than initiators expect. ([Liu et al., 11 July 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35816566/)) The strongest case against it is operational: appreciation was measured, but replies, booked calls, commercial outcomes, and repeated-contact fatigue were not.

Resolution: maintain separate relational and commercial lanes. A no-agenda greeting must remain no-agenda after the reply. Otherwise it becomes a bait-and-switch.

2. Ask a question versus make a statement

The pro-question case is that follow-up questions improve liking during conversation. ([Huang et al., 27 April 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28447835/)) The counterargument is that every question creates response work, and no dormant-DM experiment demonstrates that a question raises first-response rates. [no source found]

Resolution:

- Commercial/current-state touch: one easy, bounded question.
- Goodwill touch: usually no question; “no reply needed.”
- Never stack three questions.

3. Voice is warmer versus voice is burdensome

Voice conveys paralinguistic information and has produced greater connection than text in controlled comparisons. ([Kumar & Epley, 10 September 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32915017/)) But an unsolicited voice note requires linear listening, cannot be scanned discreetly, and has no controlled first-recontact evidence. [no source found]

Resolution: relationship-specific norm beats universal channel ideology. Text first unless voice was already native to the dyad.

4. Personalization versus surveillance

Relevant activity information revived ties in one platform experiment. ([ICWSM study, 2013](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICWSM/article/download/14390/14239/17908)) The reconnection-process research also documents that unexpected personal knowledge can terminate a reconnection. ([Rondi, Levin & De Massis, 24 May 2023](https://eprints.lancs.ac.uk/196191/1/OS_DEF.pdf))

Resolution: personalize from the shared thread, direct disclosure, or plainly public professional information. Do not reveal the breadth of monitoring.

5. AI improves drafting versus AI lowers authenticity

AI assistance can improve speed, linguistic positivity, and service engagement in some settings. A January 2026 randomized field experiment in online customer service found AI suggestions improved agent responsiveness and customer engagement, especially for less-experienced agents; that context was inbound service, not dormant relationships. ([Zhang & Narayandas, January 2026](https://ideas.repec.org/a/inm/ormnsc/v72y2026i1p73-95.html)) [single-source]

The opposing evidence is that perceived AI authorship can lower authenticity, particularly for emotional content. ([Kirk & Givi, 1 October 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbusres.2024.114984); [Zhang, Liu & Li, 29 December 2025](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/1369118X.2025.2606100))

Resolution: use AI as analyst and editor, not as the source of claimed memory, affection, gratitude, apology, or personal intent.

D. Best Practices

1. The first-message decision gate

Do not draft until all six answers are known:

- Why this person?
- Why now?
- What shared fact proves legitimate recognition?
- What may have changed since the last conversation?
- What is the smallest useful reply?
- Is there any debt, conflict, opt-out, sensitive event, or surveillance risk?

If “why now?” is empty, classify the intended touch:

- Commercial: wait.
- Relational: send only if the thought is genuine, personal, rare, and agenda-free.
- Institutional update: send only through an expected or opted-in format.

2. Anchor-selection score

Score each candidate anchor from 0–2 on:

- Provenance: is it definitely from the shared relationship or public professional record?
- Current relevance: is it likely still meaningful now?
- Recipient value: does engaging help them, not only the founder?
- Reply ease: can they answer in one line?
- Creep risk: 0 = high risk, 2 = clearly safe.

Prefer an anchor scoring at least 8/10. This threshold is an operational safeguard, not experimentally validated.

Anchor priority:

- Repaired obligation. If the founder failed to do something, lead with ownership and completed restitution. Trust-repair experiments found restitution could outperform apology alone after a psychological-contract breach, although this was not a dormant-DM study. ([Gillespie et al., 2020 issue](https://ideas.repec.org/a/kap/jbuset/v161y2020i1d10.1007_s10551-018-3984-1.html)) [single-source]
- Their explicit open loop: problem, project, decision, or question.
- Shared work or event with present relevance.
- Recipient-created present trigger.
- Legitimate mutual-person context.
- Founder’s concrete update.
- Relational memory with no ask.

3. Wording rules

Recommended default:

“[Recognition]. [Current relevance or update]. [One bounded reply path].”

Use:

- Two to four short sentences.
- One anchor.
- One present-tense delta.
- No more than one question.
- Plain language.
- The same language and level of formality previously used.
- The existing Telegram/WhatsApp thread.

Avoid:

- “Just checking in.”
- Long autobiographical updates.
- Multiple offers or questions.
- “Can I pick your brain?”
- Pretending the relationship was closer than it was.
- Transcript-like quotation.
- Apologizing merely because time passed.
- A calendar link in the first message.
- A hidden pitch after a sentimental opener.

The length and same-thread recommendations are operational rather than experimentally established. [no source found]

4. Ten opener skeletons

These are evidence-informed implementations, not head-to-head winners.

1. Their problem, with a current trigger

EN: “Maya — I remembered our conversation at [event] about [problem]. [Concrete development] just changed, and it made me wonder about [project]. Is [A] still the live issue, or has it shifted to [B]?”

RU: “Маша, вспомнил наш разговор на [событии] про [проблему]. Сейчас изменилось [конкретное изменение], и я подумал о вашем [проекте]. Узкое место всё ещё [A] или уже [B]?”

Best for: genuine external or internal delta.

2. Their old question, now answerable

EN: “We left your question about [topic] open. I finally have a real answer: [one-sentence answer]. If it is still relevant, I can send the three-bullet version here—useful?”

RU: “У нас остался открытым ваш вопрос про [тема]. Теперь у меня есть нормальный ответ: [одно предложение]. Если ещё актуально, могу прислать сюда версию в трёх пунктах — полезно?”

Best for: evidence, product result, or experience acquired since the conversation.

3. An unkept promise, now repaired

EN: “I told you I would [promise] and didn’t. That was on me. I’ve now [completed remedy]. If it is still useful, I’ll send [specific item]; if not, no need to reopen it.”

RU: “Я обещал [обещание] и не сделал. Это моя ошибка. Сейчас я [конкретно исправил]. Если ещё полезно, пришлю [результат]; если уже нет — возвращаться к этому не нужно.”

Best for: only after a real remedy exists.

4. Pure relational memory

EN: “[Object/place/event] reminded me of [specific shared moment]. I still use your point about [idea]. No agenda and no reply needed—just wanted to say hello and hope [specific project] is going well.”

RU: “[Место/событие] напомнило мне наш [конкретный эпизод]. Я до сих пор вспоминаю твою мысль про [идея]. Без повестки и отвечать не обязательно — просто хотел поздороваться и пожелать удачи с [проект].”

Best for: legitimate no-delta goodwill.

5. Public role change

EN: “Congratulations on [role/change]. We met through [context] when you were at [old organization]; your point about [idea] stayed with me. Has the move changed how you think about [bounded topic]?”

RU: “Поздравляю с [новая роль/изменение]. Мы познакомились через [контекст], когда вы были в [организация]; я запомнил вашу мысль про [идея]. После перехода взгляд на [узкая тема] изменился?”

Best for: senior operators, investors, or people with a clear public professional transition.

6. Specific opinion request

EN: “I’m choosing between [A] and [B] on [difficult topic], and you have unusually relevant experience in [domain]. One-line instinct: A or B? No need for a call.”

RU: “Выбираю между [A] и [B] в вопросе [тема], а у вас здесь редкий релевантный опыт. Какой первый инстинкт: A или B? Созвон не нужен.”

Best for: a real decision and a real expert.

7. Information gift

EN: “You once mentioned [problem]. One new data point changed my view: [one sentence]. If that problem is still live, I can send the source here; otherwise ignore.”

RU: “Вы когда-то упоминали [проблема]. Один новый факт изменил мой взгляд: [одно предложение]. Если тема ещё жива, пришлю источник сюда; если нет — можно проигнорировать.”

Best for: a genuinely high-signal resource. The summary itself must carry value; do not use the link as clickbait.

8. Former customer

EN: “When we worked on [project], [outcome or unresolved issue] was the sticking point. We have since changed [specific capability/process]. Is that problem still relevant at [company], or did it disappear?”

RU: “Когда мы работали над [проект], главным узким местом был [вопрос]. С тех пор мы конкретно изменили [возможность/процесс]. Эта проблема у [компания] ещё жива или уже исчезла?”

Best for: prior commercial relationship without unresolved harm.

9. Mutual person

EN: “[Name] came up while [legitimate context], which reminded me of our work on [specific topic]. I realized I never learned where [project] landed. Did you end up choosing [A] or [B]?”

RU: “[Имя] всплыл в разговоре про [легитимный контекст], и я вспомнил нашу работу над [тема]. Я так и не узнал, чем закончился [проект]. В итоге выбрали [A] или [B]?”

Best for: natural context only; not borrowed status or disclosure of private information.

10. Long-gap warm re-introduction

EN: “We spoke in [month/year] through [context] about [topic]—I was [old role], and I’m now [current role]. I’m resurfacing because [specific delta]. Is [topic] still on your map, or no longer a priority?”

RU: “Мы общались в [месяц/год] через [контекст] про [тема] — тогда я занимался [старая роль], сейчас [новая роль]. Пишу снова, потому что [конкретное изменение]. Эта тема ещё в приоритете или уже нет?”

Best for: more than 12–24 months, a weak original tie, or material role changes.

11. Shared-humor callback

EN: “[Shared joke] finally proved you right: [brief development]. It reminded me of [project/event]. Are you still working on [bounded topic]?”

RU: “[Общая шутка] наконец доказала, что ты был прав: [краткое изменение]. Сразу вспомнил [проект/событие]. Ты всё ещё занимаешься [узкая тема]?”

Best for: only where that style was already mutual. Humor’s upside and downside both depend heavily on appropriateness. ([Bitterly, Brooks & Schweitzer, 10 November 2016](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/Bitterly%20Brooks%20Schweitzer%20JPSP%202016_54efbab5-2561-4408-b008-38d958e0ad50.pdf)) [single-source]

No controlled RU/CIS-specific evidence was found comparing dormant warm-contact openers in Russian-language Telegram or WhatsApp. [no source found] The Russian versions above are localization patterns, not claims of distinct Russian psychology.

5. Status asymmetry

VC or senior operator:

- Re-establish context briefly.
- Show why this person, not merely “someone senior.”
- Ask for a small judgment, not open-ended mentoring.
- Make declining effortless.
- Do not overstate closeness, praise excessively, or imply entitlement to time.

Peer engineer:

- Lead with the technical problem, artifact, or result.
- Default to async.
- Offer a reproducible example, short document, repository issue, or three bullets.
- Suggest a call only after identifying complexity that written exchange cannot efficiently resolve.

Former customer:

- Name the prior outcome honestly.
- If there was a failure, repair before pitching.
- Ask whether the old problem still exists rather than assuming it does.
- Do not present ordinary product development as a personal favor.

These are operational applications of the finding that present status, tie strength, and conflict alignment matter strongly during reconnection. ([Rondi, Levin & De Massis, 24 May 2023](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/orsc.2023.1685)) [single-source]

6. Timing and channel

No reliable experiment was found identifying an optimal weekday or hour for dormant warm Telegram/WhatsApp messages. [no source found]

Recommended order:

1. Send promptly after a legitimate trigger, while the connection is intelligible.
2. Otherwise use the recipient’s historical reply window, if known without invasive monitoring.
3. Otherwise send during the recipient’s local workday.
4. Avoid time-sensitive personal events unless the recipient disclosed them directly or publicly and contact would plainly be welcome.

Use the last successful private channel. Move channels only if the prior one is unavailable, the recipient has requested another channel, or a positive reply creates a reason to do so.

7. The next two moves

Move 2: Recalibrate before converting

When they reply substantively:

- Answer what they actually said.
- Reciprocate with one relevant update.
- Correct the mental model: “That makes sense—so the priority is now X rather than Y?”
- Do not insert a calendar link simply because they replied.

When they give a polite closure such as “Great to hear from you” with no information, question, or opening:

- Treat it as acknowledgment, not revived intent.
- Either close warmly or ask one genuinely bounded follow-up.
- Do not escalate to a call.

When there is no reply:

- Do not send an empty bump.
- Send again only when a genuinely new trigger, answer, or repaired obligation exists.
- A deadline-related owed item can justify a short follow-up; mere desire for a reply cannot.

Move 3: Convert to the smallest mutually useful next step

Prefer, in order:

1. Answer in the current thread.
2. Permission to send three bullets or a short artifact.
3. A specific introduction.
4. A document, issue, or comment for async review.
5. An explicit revisit date.
6. A short call, only if synchronous discussion has a clear advantage.

Call invitation:

“This may be easier in 15 minutes. Want to compare notes? I can do Tuesday 14:00 or Thursday 11:30 London; equally happy to keep it here.”

Do not push for a call when:

- The reply is merely polite.
- They provide no reciprocal question or detail.
- The request can be answered in one or two messages.
- They explicitly signal busyness.
- Their established working style is async.
- The founder cannot name what the call would resolve.
- The primary objective is simply to restore goodwill.

E. Failure Modes

1. Treating recognition as consent

A prior conversation gives permission to recognize the person, not indefinite permission to pitch, monitor, or repeatedly contact them.

2. Overclaiming closeness

Language such as “old friend,” “we should finally reconnect,” or “as always” is damaging if the previous tie was light. Current tie-alignment was the strongest factor in the principal reconnection experiment. ([Rondi, Levin & De Massis, 24 May 2023](https://eprints.lancs.ac.uk/196191/1/OS_DEF.pdf)) [single-source]

3. Apology without repair

“I’m sorry I disappeared” is usually unnecessary when nobody owed anything. When there was an actual broken promise, apology without remedy can look like an attempt to discharge guilt onto the recipient. Trust-repair evidence favors concrete restitution alongside or beyond words. ([Gillespie et al., 2020 issue](https://ideas.repec.org/a/kap/jbuset/v161y2020i1d10.1007_s10551-018-3984-1.html)) [single-source]

4. Emotional bait-and-switch

A sentimental “thought of you” followed immediately by a sales request consumes the very appreciation that makes genuine reaching out work. The direct cost of this sequence has not been quantified. [no source found]

5. Surveillance personalization

Avoid mentioning:

- activity across multiple groups;
- private relationship or health events obtained indirectly;
- granular profile changes;
- message-read behavior;
- inferred financial or employment distress.

The best direct reconnection evidence contains a case where unexpectedly deep personal knowledge ended the exchange. ([Rondi, Levin & De Massis, 24 May 2023](https://eprints.lancs.ac.uk/196191/1/OS_DEF.pdf)) [single-source]

6. AI confabulation

The most dangerous AI error is not “sounding robotic”; it is confidently attaching the wrong project, promise, person, or sentiment to the contact. No field benchmark quantifies this specific dormant-contact risk. [no source found]

7. Automatic call conversion

Catching up increased willingness to help but did not by itself significantly increase willingness to meet in the closest controlled reconnection study. ([Rondi, Levin & De Massis, 24 May 2023](https://eprints.lancs.ac.uk/196191/1/OS_DEF.pdf)) [single-source]

8. Over-touching and platform risk

Telegram states that recipients can report unwanted private messages, including unsolicited “hello” messages; repeated violations can produce progressively longer or permanent restrictions, and restricted accounts may be unable to message people who have not saved their number. ([Telegram Spam FAQ, undated, accessed 24 August 2026](https://telegram.org/faq_spam?setln=en)) [single-source]

WhatsApp’s Business Messaging Policy requires appropriate opt-in, respect for opt-outs, and avoidance of surprising or spam-like contact; Business Platform conversations initiated outside the customer-service window require approved templates. ([WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy, undated, accessed 24 August 2026](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy)) [single-source] Meta also announced marketing-message limits, easier blocking/reporting, paid business broadcasts, and enforcement against repeat violations in April 2025. ([Meta, 3 April 2025](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/04/ways-to-manage-your-businesses-chats-on-whatsapp/)) [single-source]

These business rules do not all apply identically to an ordinary personal account, but recipient blocks and reports remain a meaningful safety signal. No study was found that quantifies reputational spillover from over-touching inside a small founder, investor, or engineering community. [no source found]

F. Strategic Recommendations

1. Adopt a dual-lane contact policy

Commercial lane:

- Requires a delta, trigger, repaired obligation, relevant question, or concrete offer.
- May progress toward a call if reciprocity and synchronous value appear.

Relational lane:

- May use a genuine memory without new business information.
- Contains no commercial ask.
- Normally contains “no reply needed.”
- Cannot be converted into a pitch during the same exchange unless the recipient independently takes it there.

Confidence: [emerging]. The distinction reconciles experimental appreciation evidence with the absence of conversion evidence.

2. Separate relationship state from opportunity state

A person can be:

- relationship warm, opportunity absent;
- relationship dormant, opportunity active;
- relationship revived, call inappropriate;
- relationship weak, trigger strong.

Do not let a positive social reply automatically update commercial intent.

3. Make “current-state correction” mandatory

Every commercial recontact after six months should give the recipient an easy way to say that the old topic has changed:

“Is this still relevant, or has the priority moved elsewhere?”

This operationalizes the strongest current reconnection factor: a mutually accurate understanding of the present relationship and context. ([Rondi, Levin & De Massis, 24 May 2023](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/orsc.2023.1685)) [single-source]

4. Use AI for preparation, not relational authorship

AI may:

- summarize the last substantive exchange;
- extract explicit projects, questions, promises, and preferences;
- distinguish direct evidence from inference;
- suggest three possible anchors;
- draft short variants;
- check question count, length, formality, and link/calendar violations;
- detect duplicate or excessive contact attempts.

The founder must:

- select the anchor;
- verify every remembered fact;
- decide whether the contact is relational or commercial;
- supply the true “why now”;
- write or materially edit the emotional sentence;
- choose the ask;
- approve messages involving status-heavy, sensitive, damaged, or personally important ties.

5. Do not institutionalize no-news DMs

For broad network maintenance, use opt-in public content, a useful newsletter, a real event, or visible community participation. Direct DMs should remain scarce enough to retain interpersonal meaning.

Relationship-intelligence products such as Affinity emphasize recency, frequency, relationship strength, and warm-introduction paths; personal CRM tools such as Dex emphasize remembered context and reminders. These are product representations, not controlled evidence that scheduled messages work. ([Affinity, undated, accessed 24 August 2026](https://www.affinity.co/product/relationship-intelligence); [Dex, undated, accessed 24 August 2026](https://getdex.com/)) [low-authority]

Signal-based sales tools similarly recommend reviving old opportunities when there is a change such as a new executive, funding, website activity, or prior-champion movement. That supports trigger-first workflow design but remains vendor practice rather than causal research. ([UserGems, undated, accessed 24 August 2026](https://www.usergems.com/playbooks/revive-closed-lost-opportunities)) [low-authority]

G. Action Plan

1. Implement the standing rule

For each contact, produce:

- Last meaningful interaction.
- Past tie strength.
- Exact shared anchor and provenance.
- Any outstanding obligation or negative ending.
- Current trigger/delta.
- Status category: senior/VC, peer, former customer, other.
- Silence band.
- Preferred channel and historical tone.
- Smallest useful response.
- Relational or commercial lane.
- Send, wait, repair first, or suppress.

2. Adopt stop rules

No credible experiment establishes an optimal number of dormant warm Telegram/WhatsApp touches. [no source found] Use the following as governance limits:

- Stop immediately after any opt-out, block, hostile response, or clear non-interest.
- A no-delta goodwill touch gets no follow-up.
- A polite reply without reciprocity does not justify a call.
- Never send an empty bump.
- Send a second message only with a new delta or fulfillment of an explicit obligation.
- Three eligible attempts are a ceiling, not a target.
- After two unanswered messages without a recipient-created trigger, suppress the contact.
- Re-entry after suppression requires a materially new reason.

3. Measure the correct funnel

No defensible public benchmark was found for dormant, previously warm 1:1 Telegram or WhatsApp reply rates. Vendor cold-email, bulk-SMS, and customer-support rates are not valid substitutes. [no source found]

Track:

- Any reply within seven days.
- Substantive reply: answer, update, question, or new information.
- Reciprocal signal: they ask something, volunteer detail, or propose a next step.
- Revival: a second meaningful exchange or agreed action within thirty days.
- Async conversion: artifact accepted, answer supplied, introduction made, or revisit date agreed.
- Call conversion.
- Negative signal: opt-out, block, report, irritation, correction of overclaimed closeness.
- Sixty- and ninety-day relationship or commercial outcome.

A no-response message is a failed message, not proof that the relationship is dead.

4. Run a controlled internal test

Start with 100–200 human-reviewed messages to establish safety and a baseline. Then test:

- Anchor: their problem versus shared project versus founder delta.
- Ending: bounded question versus low-pressure statement.
- Stratification: senior/VC, peer, former customer.
- Silence: 1–6, 6–12, 12–24, and 24+ months.
- Keep channel, length, offer, and send window as constant as practicable.

Primary outcome: substantive reply within seven days.

Guardrails:

- Negative-signal rate.
- Factual-correction rate.
- Number of replies that are merely polite.
- AI-draft rejection rate by the founder.
- Call invitations accepted versus async alternatives accepted.

Do not optimize on raw reply rate alone; a question can generate replies while lowering relationship quality or producing no useful continuation.

5. Decision options

Option 1 — Strict Delta-Only

Send only when there is a trigger, new information, completed repair, or real question.

Trade-off: lowest noise and platform/reputation risk; misses legitimate goodwill touches and serendipitous reconnection.

Option 2 — Dual-Lane Reconnection

Use delta-only rules for commercial contact, plus rare no-agenda relational messages that never conceal a pitch.

Trade-off: captures both instrumental and relational value; requires disciplined classification and human approval.

Option 3 — Programmatic Rhythm

Schedule seasonal, anniversary, quarterly, or annual messages across the dormant network.

Trade-off: scalable and measurable, but has the weakest direct evidence and the highest risk of generic language, AI-authenticity penalties, fatigue, blocks, and community-level reputation damage.

Recommendation: Option 2 — Dual-Lane Reconnection [emerging].

Main assumption: the founder and assistant can reliably keep relational touches agenda-free, verify the shared anchor, and prevent automatic escalation from a friendly reply to a sales call. If that discipline cannot be enforced, use Option 1.
