# Insight (DR DR26-07-02-HUB-07): Beyond Canton: fastest-accessible non-Canton crypto/fintech money pools for a fund-less BD team, H2 2026

> Which crypto/fintech money pools outside Canton Network are richest, fastest to access (60-90 days), and best-fit for a fund-less team selling distribution/BD/AI-enabled execution rather than regulated intermediation, and how should effort be allocated across them.

## Ключевые выводы
- Stablecoin payments infrastructure is the single biggest non-Canton pool: Fireblocks reports 88% of financial institutions have committed or will commit 2026 digital-asset budget, 53% spending $1M+; MiCA fully live in EU since July 1 2026 and GENIUS Act signed in US turned regulation from blocker into build-spec.
- Incumbents are buying into stablecoin infra rather than waiting: Mastercard's acquisition of BVNK for up to $1.8B, Circle selling CPN Managed Payments to PSPs/banks/fintechs, Stripe/Bridge already powering money-app/card products like Phantom.
- Foundation-led ecosystem programs beat governance-heavy grant systems for speed: Solana Foundation grants run ~1 week review / ~3 weeks decision, 500+ projects funded, $100M+ total; Base funded 50+ teams via Base Batches and orients around payments/markets/agents; Sui offers direct strategic investment + RFPs + DeFi Moonshots up to $500k liquidity incentives; Monad allocated ~38.5% of initial MON supply to Ecosystem Development plus Monad Momentum incentive matching.
- TON/Telegram has the largest distribution (1B+ Telegram reach, 25+ active programs via The Open Platform) but the least transparent 2026 grant ticket sizes vs Solana/Sui/Avalanche - better framed as a commercial/venture-builder wedge than a grant wedge.
- RWA/tokenization is already large: RWA.xyz shows $30.87B distributed asset value, $398.59B represented asset value, $14.60B tokenized US Treasuries; Securitize is the leading platform and Circle is bridging stablecoins to tokenized treasuries.
- Institutional custody/compliance vendors have real funded demand but early-stage market: only 16% of institutions have reached production despite committed budgets, creating an open 'education + selection + deployment' window, sharpened by MiCA's July 1 2026 full application.
- Ethereum L2 grant ecosystems (Arbitrum, Optimism) are cooling as a business model: grant surfaces mix active/inactive/complete programs, governance redesign and paused retro-funding make them slower and less reliable than Solana/Sui/Base/Monad/Avalanche.
- Other cooling narratives: Base ended its creator-rewards program in Feb 2026 after ~$450k to 17,000+ creators (pivoted to trading-first model); generic GPU/DePIN aggregation is saturated (Aethir $100M fund, io.net, Akash already dominant) leaving only verticalized/service-layer niches; pure token-raise/capital-intro brokerage is weakening under tightened regulation.
- Prediction markets show real but fragile money: monthly volume rose from <$5B (mid-2025) to ~$24B (April 2026), Polymarket topped $1B annualized revenue, Kalshi runs a live market-maker program - but concentrated in sports and exposed to regulatory/oracle risk, so best treated as a sponsor-room niche not a core bet.
- DAO/token-team treasury operations remain a real budget line (DAO treasuries >$26B per Eco's 2026 guide; Compound running a $30M treasury program; Karpatkey/Steakhouse/Gauntlet occupy paid mandates) but a non-licensed small team can only credibly sell policy/reporting/dashboarding/vendor-selection work, not AUM management.

## Рекомендации / решения
- Reallocate effort so Canton is ~35-40% of BD focus (not 50%), spreading the rest across: stablecoin-rails wedge 25-30%, Base/Solana/Sui/Monad ecosystem-access wedge 20-25%, TON/Telegram commercialization 10-15%, RWA/institutional-infra/treasury sidecar 10-15%.
- Build a 'Stablecoin Rails Launch Desk': corridor maps, partner shortlists, buyer intro sequencing, operator briefings, AI-enabled outbound; price as $25k-40k 4-week launch sprints or $12k-20k/mo retainers, plus $7k-20k sponsor-funded operator rooms, leading with a Europe/MiCA angle.
- Build a 'Land Here in 30 Days' multi-ecosystem access desk covering Base/Solana/Sui/Monad: ecosystem-fit decision memo, grant/incentive routing, intro sets, announcement plan; price $15k-30k sprint converting to $10k-18k/mo retainers.
- Run TON/Telegram Operator Briefings (one sponsor at a time, $5k-20k per briefing) paired with commercial-partnership sprints ($10k-20k/mo), leveraging the existing Telegram CRM/graph rather than chasing TON's opaque grant menu.
- Avoid framing the business as 'helping crypto projects' - reframe as 'helping payments, treasury, and digital-asset operators ship faster' since real budget owners are banks/PSPs/fintechs/payment incumbents, not crypto-native teams.
- Do not anchor on Ethereum L2 grant-farming (Arbitrum/Optimism), generic AI-crypto narrative chasing, consumer-social/creator-rewards crypto, generic GPU aggregation, or pure token-raise capital-intro work - all identified as cooled/weak wedges.
- Sell 'treasury ops without custody' (policy memos, manager RFPs, KPI dashboards, reporting automation, governance materials) at $8k-20k/mo as the DAO/treasury entry product instead of AUM-based treasury management.
- Position ecosystem pitches around off-chain ROI (launch velocity, partner density, credible operators, proof of usage) rather than generic community-growth narratives, matching how Solana/Sui/Monad programs actually evaluate applicants.

## Открытые вопросы
- TON's actual 2026 grant ticket sizes remain publicly opaque compared to Solana/Sui/Avalanche - needs direct verification with TON/TOP program operators.
- Service-retainer price ranges ($10k-50k+/mo web3 BD/PR/tokenomics benchmarks) are triangulated from public agency pricing, not hard market averages - confidence is medium, not high.
- L2 governance funding surfaces (Arbitrum, Optimism) are internally contradictory, mixing historical/inactive/rolling programs - unclear how much real near-term money remains accessible.
- Whether Monad's large ecosystem allocation converts into fast accessible cash checks or mostly matching-incentive/co-marketing support is not yet confirmed mechanically.
