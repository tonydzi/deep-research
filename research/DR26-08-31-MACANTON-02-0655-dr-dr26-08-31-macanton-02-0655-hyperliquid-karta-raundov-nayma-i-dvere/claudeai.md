A. Executive summary (as of 31 Aug 2026)

The Hyperliquid stack has split into three layers with very different capital-formation logic: (1) Hyperliquid Labs / Hyper Foundation, still 11-ish people, no VC, no marketing, hiring only engineers; (2) HIP-3 deployers and builder-code frontends, which are now where the volume, the revenue and the venture money sit; (3) HyperEVM DeFi (LSTs, lending, CDPs, yield), which raised small seed rounds in 2025–early 2026 and is now pivoting to institutional/permissioned products. The realistic door for a capital-formation/ecosystem-BD profile is not the core team; it is layer (2) and the institutional-wrapper layer around it (prime brokers, custodians, DATs, HIP-3 deployers, LST institutional desks). Verified BD/institutional job postings were scarce; most "BD need" evidence below is inferred from product launches, not from ads, and is labelled as such.

E. How institutional onboarding actually works now

1. Prime-broker / OEMS rails (the real door for hedge funds): Ripple Prime integrated Hyperliquid on Feb 4 2026 — clients keep a single counterparty, consolidated margining and portfolio-level risk, and can cross-margin DeFi exposures with FX or fixed income. Talos added Hyperliquid on May 26 2026, including HIP-3 equities/commodities/indices, inside existing Talos workflows. FalconX (confidential S-1, Cantor advising) is the other prime broker serving hedge funds and market makers; Coinbase Prime is the comparison set.
2. Custody + staking: BitGo qualified custody for HyperEVM since Aug 2025; Anchorage powers HYPE ETF custody/settlement for 21Shares, Bitwise and Grayscale, runs one of the largest validators, and lets clients liquid-stake via Kinetiq from the custody account. Bitwise BHYP (first US spot HYPE ETF with in-house staking) launched May 15 2026.
3. Vaults / HLP: HLP has a 4-day lockup, no performance fee, and is fully community-owned; with order-book liquidity now mature, HLP yields have fallen to near zero and the next upgrade routes idle USDC into a lending sub-strategy for Portfolio Margin traders. User vaults carry a fixed 10% performance fee to the leader. Institutionally, HLP is now a liquidation buffer, not a yield product.
4. Builder codes and frontends: a protocol-level parameter lets interfaces append a builder address for automated onchain fee capture; >$65M has been paid to third-party developers; the structure mimics US equities — brokers own the client, wholesalers execute. Phantom did $37B perp volume in under a year via a wallet tab. Arete's thesis: fintechs/brokers will integrate builder codes directly.
5. HIP-3 / HIP-4 deployment (capital-markets door): stake 500k HYPE (~$25–33M, slashable) to deploy three perp markets, then bid in Dutch auctions; deployers earn 50% of fees. HIP-4 outcome markets went live May 2 2026 and collateralize in USDH. HIP-3 now drives ~65% of volume on a 7-day basis, with extreme concentration in trade.xyz. Aligned quote tokens (USDH) get 20% lower taker and 50% higher maker rebates.
6. Market-maker programs: none exist at the core. Yan's Jan 2024 statement stands: no investors, no paid market makers, no team commissions, no insiders. MM incentives are therefore set by deployers and frontends, not by Hyperliquid.
7. Listed wrappers: Hyperion DeFi gates institutional pools through HiHYPE minted via the Kinetiq x Hyperion validator, with the stated aim of pulling institutional-grade capital into HyperEVM.
8. Regulatory overhang to price in: the UK FCA labelled Hyperliquid and Hyper Foundation as unauthorised (Jun 2026); revenue fell ~43% from the 2025 peak while HIP-3 pass-through grows.

- VC money: still no. Self-funded from Chameleon Trading profits; Yan: if Bitcoin had taken a VC round, it would not be Bitcoin. Unchanged through 2026.
- External hires: yes, but engineering only, olympiad-level, in-person Singapore; Yan wants to hire more and added two after a spring 2026 visit. There is no marketing team and no ecosystem-BD function. Yan explicitly wants Hyperliquid to be a protocol layer where others build stablecoins, tokenized assets and apps — i.e. BD is outsourced to the ecosystem by design.

- Conclusion: the only "door" into the core is engineering or a validator seat. For a capital-formation/BD profile the door is the layer above.
