## Does core still refuse VC money and external hires?

The VC answer is yes: Hyperliquid Labs still presents itself as self-funded with no external capital, and the Foundation states “No investors” and “No paid market makers.” [SEC description](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2107730/000119312526237193/hype_s-1_amendment_3.htm) [Foundation](https://www.hyperfoundation.org/)

The hiring answer is no: “no external hires” is not accurate because Labs publicly recruits external engineers. What remains true is that the visible organization is technically concentrated and does not currently expose a conventional institutional-sales, partnerships or capital-markets hiring funnel. [Labs jobs](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/Hyperliquid%20Labs) [Backend role](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/Hyperliquid%20Labs/08060480-1355-490c-b765-97ea1b529ac6)

The realistic path for a capital-formation/ecosystem-BD profile is therefore not “raise money for Hyperliquid” or “sell preferential MM access.” Hyperliquid neither needs venture capital nor offers special DMM economics; the valuable work is assembling capital, custody, credit, market-maker flow and distribution around permissionless protocol rails.
